# Third-party methodology and tooling

- [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) — MIT. Used as the normative R&D and verification methodology. Not vendored.
- [getsentry/MobileBuildMCP](https://github.com/getsentry/MobileBuildMCP) — MIT. Used as the Apple build/test/device MCP and CLI backend. Not vendored; package version is pinned in `integrations/mobilebuildmcp.yaml`.

Their names and links identify upstream projects; this repository is not affiliated with their maintainers.
