# Definitions of done

## Discovery

Adapter, source paths, test paths, conventions, at least one build command, and critical unknowns are recorded.

## Development

Planned steps are complete or explicitly deferred, changes fit the scope budget, fast checks pass, and deviations are recorded.

## Verification

Every profile-required check has evidence and no blocking verification finding remains.

## Review

No critical or high finding remains. Medium findings are fixed, accepted, or deferred with a reason.

## QA

Required scenarios were executed, skips are explained, evidence is linked, and no blocking defect remains.

## Final

The decision is derived from stage results; it is never entered as an unsupported assertion.
